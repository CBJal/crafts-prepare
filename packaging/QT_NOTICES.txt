Qt, PySide6, and the playback libraries
======================================

Crafts Prepare 0.2.0 links to Qt 6 through PySide6. The packages are
PySide6-Essentials, PySide6-Addons, and shiboken6, pinned in
backend/requirements.txt. Qt WebEngine is not installed and is not part of
this application. There is no embedded browser.

Dynamic linking and relinking
-----------------------------

The Windows installer is a PyInstaller onedir folder. Qt, shiboken, and the
Qt Multimedia libraries are separate shared libraries next to
CraftsPrepare.exe. They are not compiled into that executable.

You may replace those shared libraries with compatible builds you produce
from the corresponding source, or rebuild this application against another
PySide6 build. Replacing a library does not require Crafts Prepare's own
source to be relicensed.

Qt Multimedia is not the FFmpeg you configure
---------------------------------------------

Playback can use Qt Multimedia's FFmpeg plugin. That plugin loads separate
shared libraries such as avcodec, avformat, swscale, and swresample that ship
inside the Qt build, together with the ffmpegmediaplugin. Those files are
only the playback backend.

They are not ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe, or Gyroflow. Export, proxies, stills,
and gyro stabilization call the programs you install and set under Tools.
If those programs are missing, the job fails and is not reported as success.
A missing playback codec does not change that.

The Qt playback libraries are their own FFmpeg build. Their source is the
FFmpeg revision Qt used for Qt 6.12, published with the Qt sources above.
Crafts Prepare does not grant any extra right to redistribute a separately
downloaded FFmpeg or Gyroflow.
